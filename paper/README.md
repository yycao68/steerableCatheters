## Key editorial decisions vs. the markdown draft

1. **Theorems via `amsthm`.** The md's "Theorem 1–3" become `\begin{theorem}`
   environments (Theorem 1 impedance equivalence, Theorem 2 nominal stability,
   Theorem 3 ISS/offset-free); Remarks 1–2 become numbered `remark` environments.
   The base-framework results are cited as results of [13], not re-numbered here.
2. **All tables numbered.** The md's prose "Role of MPC Prediction" table is promoted
   to a numbered Table I; the four-controller comparison becomes Table II, capability
   comparison Table III, quantitative comparison Table IV, and the safety-mode-under-
   cardiac-motion test Table V (booktabs throughout).
3. **Measured-only claims preserved.** The evidence boundaries of the md are kept
   in substance: integral compensation is characterized in free space, the
   corrective-force limit is evaluated separately, and force-regulation mode gives
   141.9 mN (static) / 166.6 mN (cardiac) RMSE. The hardware results in [14], [15]
   are presented as different-platform context. Projected quantities are separated from
   measured results. The force-limited position-tracking mode is separately shown to hold the
   0.5 N bound under moderate (0.3 mm/1 Hz) cardiac motion and to breach it only at
   the contact-onset impact transient for a fast/large (0.5 mm/1.2 Hz) wall — the same
   environment-induced effect as the approach-velocity limit (Table V, Fig. 3).
4. **Reference list trimmed to cited works** (15 + OSQP). The md's verification-status
   note is dropped from the paper body (it is meta-commentary); citation accuracy
   should still be checked before submission — see TODO.
5. Double-anonymous: base paper [13] (Cao & Tang) cited in third person throughout;
   author block "Anonymous Author(s)" in `catheter_ieee.tex`.
6. **Force-regulation mode moved to Appendix A.** The setpoint force-regulation
   comparison (141.9 mN static / 166.6 mN cardiac vs [14], [15] hardware) is a
   supplementary characterization and was relocated out of §VI so the main
   force-limited position-tracking result remains distinct from setpoint force
   regulation. §VI keeps the force-limited cardiac-motion test (Table V, Fig. 3);
   Table IV drops the force-reg row; the "Honest reading" paragraph points to App. A.
7. **All simulation is now MuJoCo-based (full port, 2026-06-22).** §VI is run entirely on
   the distributed-compliance MuJoCo plant (8-link PRB tendon-driven catheter, analytic
   Kelvin–Voigt wall) — `../simulation/catheter_benchmark_mujoco.py`. The four-controller
  Table II, cardiac Table V, both figures, and the §V-E misalignment sweep are MuJoCo;
   App. A force-regulation is ported too. Impedance gains are Λ-scaled to the measured
   inertia (Λ≈3.5e-3) and the force bound is enforced in tendon space via J_n(κ)
   (`T = -(u_ff+F_mpc)/J_n`). The previous RK4-primary / MuJoCo-cross-validation split is
   gone: the old `sec:mujoco`/Table VI cross-validation subsection is replaced by a
   "Robustness to Unmodeled Tendon Hysteresis" subsection (the genuine remaining MuJoCo
   finding, motivating future-work (vi)). **Headline numbers changed** — on the compliant
   physics plant the unconstrained MPC peaks at 0.60 N (19% over the bound), not the RK4
   plant's 7.25 N (14×); offset-free reduction is 90% (was 98%); classical impedance now
   stays under the bound (0.17 N) by being too soft to track; cardiac motion no longer
   breaches (compliance buffers it). The old RK4 script is no longer part of the active
   reproducibility path. The pole/QP/timing checks in `catheter_verify.py` remain
   analytical/reduced-model checks; the approach-velocity check now runs on the current
   MuJoCo compliant plant and is framed as a safety qualification in §VI. **Both wrappers build to 9 pp** (after a prose-tightening pass on
   2026-06-22; the "8 pp" figure earlier in this README is stale). Reaching 8 pp would
   require cutting content (the Fig. 1 plant schematic and/or compressing the App. B LPV
   derivation) — deferred by author preference; the clean 9 pp keeps all claims, figures,
   tables, and appendices.
